# Privacy policy for Zovo Hide Shipping & Payment

Last updated 6 November 2026.

## What the app stores

The app stores the checkout rules that you create. They are saved in your own Shopify store, as app metafields on the app installation and on the two checkout customizations that the app adds. The app server keeps the Shopify session for your store so that it can call the Shopify Admin API for you.

## What the app does not store

The app does not read or store orders, customer names, emails, addresses or payment details. At checkout, Shopify runs the app's Functions inside Shopify. The Functions see the cart, the delivery address country and postal code, and whether the customer has the tags that your rules name. That data stays inside Shopify and is not sent to the app server.

## Sharing

The app shares no data with anyone. It uses no analytics or tracking tools.

## Deleting data

When you uninstall the app, the app server deletes your store session. Shopify removes the app metafields and the checkout customizations. Shopify also sends the app a shop redact request 48 hours after uninstall, and the app deletes anything left for your store.
